# License adoption checks

Updated 2026-10-18. The repository remains private. Neither this checklist nor
the revised drafts adopts a license or authorizes public distribution.

## Confirmed commercial decisions

- Preserve authentic public prices and commitments in the commercial schedule.
- Personal covers the purchased major version; Commercial includes current
  releases while active. Self-hosted users can be added without per-user fees.
- Source publication grants inspection only under the adopted source-view terms;
  operational and additional source rights require the separate applicable grant.
- Cloud terms describe the service in the present tense. New signups may remain
  closed without changing obligations under an accepted order.

## Resolve before adoption or accepting relevant orders

- Verify ownership and licensor identity; complete governing law, notices,
  consumer protections, refunds, cancellation and multi-year termination terms
  with qualified counsel. Confirm US dollars as the public dollar denomination.
- Complete installation scope and record requested user allowances without
  introducing a paid self-hosted seat tier. Confirm license issuance can support
  promised additions; technical capacity is not unlimited.
- Verify each client's read/export behavior. The draft uses the existing 30-day
  grace interval for finite self-hosted offline retrieval permission, but current
  offline clients do not implement a complete expiry lock. Implement and test any
  promised technical cutoff separately; a legal edit does not implement one.
- Align the homepage's unconditional renewal-restores-access sentence and the
  pricing page's expiry description with the qualified recovery wording when
  publishing the terms. Keep the confirmed prices and plan commitments intact.
- Complete Cloud's finite export, deletion, backup-retention and service/privacy
  schedule before accepting Cloud orders. No arbitrary Cloud retention period
  has been selected by this revision.
- Publish an accurate privacy notice for the existing access-request intake;
  Cloud signup availability does not remove this separate collection issue.
- Publish the adopted root LICENSE with the reviewed public source export in
  the same release. Link the adopted commercial terms and capture the accepted
  version and order. Finalize any future AGPL conversion separately.

## Review reference

The FTC's [advertising guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business)
explains why material public promises and qualifications should agree for
US-facing marketing. This reference does not select governing law or establish
that US law is the only applicable law. Local and customer-market review remains
part of adoption.
