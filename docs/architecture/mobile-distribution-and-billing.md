# Future mobile distribution and billing boundary

Decision direction, 2026-10-06: design Android and iOS as free vault clients that sign in to an existing Cloud tenant or self-hosted server. Subscription purchase, seat changes, invoices, and tenant control ownership live on the website/customer portal. Do not build in-app checkout into the first mobile clients. A vault user may differ from the tenant's unlicensed control owner.

This is a product architecture direction, not approval of App Store or Play Store copy. The mobile app should remain useful for already-entitled customers and self-hosted users. Avoid purchase buttons, prices, checkout web views, and an outbound purchase prompt in the app by default. A neutral account or entitlement status can be considered only after reviewing the exact store, country, app classification, and screen copy. Verify current store rules again before submission.

Apple's [App Review Guidelines 3.1.3(f)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) describe free standalone companions to paid web tools without in-app purchases or outbound purchase calls to action. Whether a password manager qualifies is subject to App Review; it is not a reader app merely because it reads vault data. The [reader app external-link entitlement](https://developer.apple.com/support/reader-apps/) is limited to specified media categories and should not be assumed available to us. Google Play [permits consumption-only apps](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10281818?hl=en), but its rules for directing users to outside payment vary by region and program. External billing/link programs can still have conditions and fees. Keeping billing on the website does not by itself guarantee zero store fees or unrestricted in-app links.

The website must clearly show $30 per licensed Cloud vault user per year, annual upfront payment, the two-year commitment, initial seat count, first-year charge, and two-year total before checkout. The waitlist can collect seat interest and display an estimate; it is not a purchase or entitlement. The billing control plane later verifies payment and binds the seat entitlement to the tenant ID. Mobile clients display server-provided entitlement status but do not enforce seat limits locally.
