POLICIES

Privacy Policy

How we handle your contact, installation and billing information.

Website, requests and support

We use the contact details and information you submit to review access requests, provide downloads and licenses, and answer support questions. We store submissions encrypted and restrict access to authorized operators. We use security cookies and request information to protect forms against abuse.

Self-hosted vaults

Your devices encrypt vault contents before synchronization. Your server stores encrypted vault records and account, device and synchronization metadata. 127Vaults uses installation identifiers and license status to deliver and renew licenses. See our security page for details.

Service providers and billing

We use providers to host and protect the site, deliver account messages and process payments. We use subscription and transaction status to manage your license and respond to billing questions.

Retention and requests

We keep records as needed to provide access and support, manage billing or meet legal obligations. Use our support form to ask about your information.

Full Privacy Policy

1. Scope

This policy describes how 127Vaults handles information from its website, customer portal and support channels, and information used to administer Self-Hosted access and billing.

2. Information you provide

When you request access, we receive your name, email address, requested plan, user count and organization where applicable. When you contact support, we receive your reply address and the information you include in your message. Use the support form for service questions and keep passwords, recovery keys, card details and vault contents out of your message.

3. Information used to operate the service

We use your information to review requests, provide invitations and downloads, authenticate customer sign-in, manage installation licenses, answer support requests and administer purchases. We use installation identifiers and license status for licensing. We also use browser and network information and cookies needed for security, abuse prevention and customer sessions.

4. Service providers and disclosures

We use service providers for hosting, website security and delivery of account and service messages. They receive information needed to perform those functions, such as the recipient address and message content for delivery. When you buy through Dodo Payments, it receives the information you enter at checkout and processes your payment. We receive purchase and subscription information needed for licensing, billing support, refunds and dispute handling. We may disclose information where required by law.

5. Self-hosted vault data

Authorized clients encrypt vault contents before synchronization. Your server stores encrypted records and account, device and synchronization metadata. Your self-hosted vault contents and the keys that decrypt them stay within your self-hosted environment and authorized clients during normal operation. The 127Vaults customer portal is separate from vault sign-in. See the security overview for the trust boundaries.

6. Retention and protection

We encrypt access and support submissions at rest and restrict access to authorized operators. We keep information as needed for access review, licensing, support, billing, disputes, security and legal obligations. Active licenses and billing records may require longer retention than an access request.

7. Privacy requests

Use Contact Support to ask about your information or request access, correction or deletion. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request. Applicable law may require us to keep some records.
