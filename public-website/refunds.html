Refunds and Cancellation

How trials end and where to get help with a payment or renewal.

Free trials

The 10-day self-hosted trial requires no payment method and ends automatically. To continue with an eligible paid offer, you must actively choose to subscribe.

Stopping a renewal

Cancel a self-serve subscription before the next billing date to stop future charges. There is no early termination fee.

Prepaid time

We review prorated refund requests when a substantial part of the paid period remains. Use the support form to request a review.

Full Refund and Cancellation Policy

1. Scope and free trials

This policy applies to self-serve Commercial Self-Hosted purchases. For negotiated contracts, the signed agreement sets the applicable terms. The 10-day trial ends automatically and carries no charge. A paid subscription starts only if you choose to purchase it.

2. How to cancel

Open Manage subscription from your private invitation and cancel renewal before the next billing date. If you cannot access the subscription page, contact support using the email associated with the purchase. Cancellation stops future charges, including future annual installments within a five- or ten-year commitment. We charge no early termination fee or balance for the unused commitment years.

3. Access after cancellation

If you cancel renewal, your license normally stays active through the period you already paid for. Cancelling renewal does not automatically issue a refund. If you also request a refund, we will confirm the outcome and any effect on your access.

4. Prorated refunds for prepaid time

We review requests individually, taking into account how much of the prepaid period has elapsed and how much remains. For example, if you cancel within the first two months of an annual period, we may refund the unused portion on a prorated basis. If only one or two months remain, we generally do not issue a prorated refund. These examples guide our review and do not create an automatic refund entitlement. This policy does not limit any refund rights you have under applicable law.

5. How to request a refund

Use Contact Support and include your purchase email, invoice reference and reason for the request. We will review the request and confirm whether a refund applies and the amount, if approved.
