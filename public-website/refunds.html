Refunds and Cancellation

How trials end and where to get help with a payment or renewal.

Free trials

The 10-day self-hosted trial requires no payment method and ends automatically. To continue with an eligible paid offer, you must actively choose to subscribe.

Stopping a renewal

When paid subscriptions are available, use Manage subscription from your private invitation to stop automatic renewal before your next billing date. Access continues through the paid period. Review your offer for any separate contract commitments before subscribing. If you cannot access the subscription page, use our support form.

Requesting a refund

Use the support form with your purchase email, invoice reference and reason for the request. We review refund requests according to the terms shown with your purchase and applicable law. Cancelling renewal does not itself refund a payment.
